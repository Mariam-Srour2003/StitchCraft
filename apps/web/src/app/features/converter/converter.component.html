What makes a photo stitch well

  • Get close. The subject should fill the frame. A face needs to be about 30 stitches tall to look like someone.
  • Keep it simple. A plain background turns into clean stitching; a busy one turns into scattered single stitches.
  • Good, even light. Dark or washed-out photos lose detail to one or two threads.
  • Sharp and in focus. Blur becomes bands of in-between colours.

After you choose a photo, the photo check tells you how well it will work and how to improve it.
